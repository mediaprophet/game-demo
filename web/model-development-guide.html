1. The Five Non-Negotiable Axioms

All virtual embodiments compiled for QualiaDB, webizen-os-shell, and game-demo must adhere to these five architectural invariants:

1. Ontological Integrity & Anti-Commodification

Entities must declare their ontological classification. Living organisms are Specimens; human subjects are sovereign Personas; manufactured tools are Constructs. Human persons can never be compiled as assets.

2. Continuous EMF Spectral Materials

No raw RGB tristimulus albedo. All optical reflectance follows the Jakob & Hanika (2019) polynomial basis $(c_0, c_1, c_2)$, guaranteeing physically bounded energy $R(\lambda) \in [0.0, 1.0]$.

3. 4-Tier Decoupled Virtual Geometry

Every entity must provide references across all 4 resolution tiers (Hero Meshlets $\to$ Decimated Mesh $\to$ Octahedral Impostor Atlas $\to$ Hierarchical Surfels) to bypass GPU quad-overdraw.

4. Deterministic Zero-Heap Packaging

Authored folders compile into sealed .10d living containers (Sections 1, 13, 17, 18, 19, 20) with per-section CRC-32C checksums and .hmc packages under the 42MB Sentinel ceiling.

5. Reverse-LOD Locality

Containers are sequenced in Reverse-LOD layout (Header $\to$ Surfels $\to$ Impostor $\to$ Decimated $\to$ Hero Meshlets) enabling instantaneous far-field display on mobile WebGPU while streaming.

4. Continuous EMF Spectral Materials & Jakob-Hanika Basis

Physical light transport simulates continuous electromagnetic radiant energy across $\lambda \in [380, 780]\text{ nm}$:

$u = \frac{\lambda - 380}{400} \in [0.0, 1.0]$
$f(u) = c_0 u^2 + c_1 u + c_2$
$R(u) = 0.5 + 0.5 \cdot \frac{f(u)}{\sqrt{1.0 + f(u)^2}}$

5. Continuous Spectral Acoustics & AudioWorklet Spatialization

Acoustic properties mirror optical reflectance in normalized logarithmic frequency space:

$u = \frac{\log_{10}(f / 20.0)}{\log_{10}(1000.0)} = \frac{\log_{10}(f / 20.0)}{3.0} \in [0.0, 1.0]$
Anchor Frequencies: Low = 200 Hz, Mid = 1000 Hz, High = 8000 Hz
Denormal protection DC offset: $x[n] = x_{\text{raw}}[n] + 10^{-24}$

Filter coefficients are smoothed linearly across 128-sample quanta ($2.67\text{ ms}$) in Direct Form II Transposed (DF2T), ensuring zero zipper noise and $< 10$ FLOPs per voice per sample.

7. Offline CLI Compilation & Verification Toolchain

To compile authored entity folders into living .10d containers and .hmc packages, execute the zero-heap Rust CLI subcommands:

PowerShell / Bash Terminal Commands 
# 1. Compile living entity model into .10d and .hmc container
qualia-cli entity-compile \
  --input ./entities/flora/eucalyptus_regnans/entity.json \
  --output ./assets/eucalyptus_regnans.10d \
  --style community \
  --reverse-lod

# 2. Cryptographically verify gates and structural integrity
qualia-cli entity-verify \
  --input ./assets/eucalyptus_regnans.10d \
  --strict-gates \
  --output-audit-receipt ./receipts/audit-euc.json

# 3. Inspect in the browser review studio with direct autoload:
# Open: ./asset-review.html?style=community&asset=10d/eucalyptus_regnans.10d&autoload=1&audit=1

8. Verification Gates Checklist (Gates 1–5)

Gate 1: Spectral Plausibility

Reflectance polynomial evaluates to $R(\lambda) \in [0.0, 1.0]$ across all wavelengths. BSSRDF mean free paths are strictly positive non-zero.

Gate 2: Geometry Confinement

Decimated LODs and impostor bounding volumes remain strictly within the Tier 1 Hero bounding volume within a 5 cm margin.

Gate 3: Ontological & Kinematics

Ontology classes resolve to graph dictionary; vertex bone weights are normalized ($\sum w_i = 1.0$) with valid skeletal joint indices.

Gate 4: Script Determinism

Wasm/VibeScript affordances execute within $1,000,000$ cycle budget with zero unseeded entropy or non-deterministic host calls.

Gate 5: Anti-Commodification

Natural persons cannot be commodified as commercial property or industrial assets. Sovereign representations require DID consent.
