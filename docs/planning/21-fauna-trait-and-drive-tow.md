# Fauna traits and the drive/tow scaffold

Status: planning scaffold only. No chrome, no mesh, no soft-rise until a
Qualia frame actually paints. Tag `v0.0.40.11` (`19e2abe`) is the QG-12
black-viewport fix; a scene receipt is not paint.

## Fauna personality

A personality is a **trait on a living non-person**. Fauna may carry one.
That trait is motion and temperament on the place. It is not a who, not a
handle, and not a face. Flora and funga stay separate living kinds; none of
them become a character or a player instrument.

## Drive and tow

The corridor between places is a mechanic, not a map texture.

- The **vehicle** and the **van** are two instruments, hitched. They are not
  one object and not a who.
- **Load** is how the mass sits on that hitch. A badly loaded van or bad
  driving can write off both instruments.
- **Overheat** and **fuel** are states of those instruments. Fuel cost is a **scenario claim on the trip**, not spending authority and not a checkout.
- A **write-off** is a receipt: both instruments end as wrecked scene state.
- A **licence** is the capacity to use the instruments. Lose it and they stay
  parked; the cell cannot take the corridor.
- A life that ends in that scene stays a **fictional scene state**. It is not
  a score, a portrait, or an instruction about a real human.

## What this document does not do

It does not add a second renderer, a second physics engine, or a vehicle
mesh. Instruments stay data on the handle until Qualia can show one accepted
mesh. Soft-rise of heat, fuel, hitch, or a fauna trait waits on that paint.
